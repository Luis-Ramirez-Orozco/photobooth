What we do

Photo booths for every event

We provide photo booth service for events of all kinds. Whether you're planning a wedding, a birthday or a company party, our booths give your guests something fun to do and give you photo strips to remember the day.

We bring everything to you. We show up an hour before your event's scheduled time and install the booth, so it's ready when your first guest walks in.

Choose our inflatable enclosed photo booth, in white or black, or our 360 photo booth, where the camera arm rotates around your guests.
